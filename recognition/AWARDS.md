## About These Competitions

### Planter de Sondeigs i Experiments
A prestigious Catalan statistics and experiments competition for secondary school students, organized by major universities and statistical institutions. The 2021 edition received 57 projects from 169 students across 21 schools in Catalonia.

### ICFO Young Photonics Congress
An annual congress organized by the Institute of Photonic Sciences (ICFO) where young researchers present innovative projects combining optics, photonics, and technology.
